C6 CODING RULES -- estimator provenance of the primary cis nomination list
Written 2026-08-26, BEFORE any paper was read for this criterion.
Not to be edited after coding starts. Deviations go in the results register.

WHY THIS CRITERION EXISTS
The paper's central claim is an identity that holds for a single-variant Wald
cis-MR. Its importance depends on how much of this literature runs that design.
We first tried to answer that by phrase matching (step151) and the attempt
failed: the count fell from 71 papers to 10 when the phrase was required to
appear in the same sentence as a cis-eQTL mention, so it measured proximity of
wording, not design. C6 replaces it with a judgement a person can make and
another person can check.

THE QUESTION
  Which estimator produced THIS paper's primary cis-eQTL target-nomination list?

"Primary" means the list the paper carries forward as its findings -- the genes
it names in the abstract or the results as its candidates. Not a sensitivity
analysis, not a trans analysis, not a replication panel.

CATEGORIES -- enter exactly one code in the `coder` column.

  W   single-variant Wald. The primary cis list rests on one instrument per
      gene, and significance therefore comes from a Wald ratio (or an IVW/median
      call that is arithmetically a Wald ratio because there is one instrument).
      Code W when the paper says one variant per gene, or reports exactly one
      SNP per exposure in its instrument table, or states that the top/lead
      cis-eQTL was used as the instrument.

  M   multi-instrument estimator. The primary cis list rests on two or more
      instruments per gene combined by IVW, weighted median, MR-Egger or a
      mode-based estimator. Code M when instrument counts per gene are plainly
      greater than one for the genes that are nominated.

  X   mixed. The primary list is produced by a pipeline in which some genes
      carry one instrument and others carry several, or the paper reports both a
      Wald and a multi-instrument result for the same list without designating
      one as primary.

  U   not ascertainable. The paper does not say, and the instrument count per
      gene cannot be recovered from the text, tables or supplement available.

DECISION ORDER -- apply in this order and stop at the first that resolves.
  1. An explicit statement of instruments per gene for the primary cis analysis.
  2. An instrument table or supplementary table from which the count per
     nominated gene can be read.
  3. An explicit statement that the estimator used for the primary cis analysis
     was the Wald ratio, with no indication of multiple instruments -> W.
  4. An explicit statement that IVW (or median/Egger/mode) produced the primary
     cis list, with an indication that instruments were multiple -> M.
  5. Otherwise -> U.

WHAT DOES NOT COUNT
  * Naming IVW anywhere in the paper. Running IVW on trans, on a relaxed set, or
    on a non-primary sensitivity analysis does not make the primary cis list M.
  * The phrase "single-variant", "single SNP" or "single-cell" anywhere in the
    text. Proximity to the cis analysis is what matters and the phrase alone
    does not establish it.
  * A methods sentence describing the general MR framework rather than what this
    paper did.
  * The number of instruments before clumping or filtering. What matters is the
    number carried into the estimate for the nominated genes.

U IS A REAL ANSWER, NOT A FAILURE
If the paper does not say and the count cannot be recovered, code U. A U rate is
itself a finding about the literature's reporting. Do not guess from the journal,
the year, the authors or how sophisticated the paper seems.

EVIDENCE IS MANDATORY
For every judgement other than U, paste into `evidence_quote` the sentence or
table caption you relied on, and put its location (section name, table number,
or "supplementary table N") in `evidence_where`. A judgement with no quote is
treated as U at adjudication.

BLINDING
The two coders work independently. Neither sees the other's file, and neither
file carries the step151 automated scan result. Rows are shuffled under
different seeds so that any ordering in the corpus is not shared.

ADJUDICATION
Disagreements are resolved jointly, never by one coder alone, and Cohen's kappa
is computed BEFORE adjudication. Both the pre-adjudication kappa and the raw
agreement are reported, because with four categories and a likely dominant one,
kappa can collapse for prevalence reasons while agreement is high -- the same
artefact already documented for C1.

WHAT WILL BE REPORTED
  n coded, raw agreement, Cohen's kappa (pre-adjudication), the adjudicated
  distribution over W / M / X / U, and a Wilson interval for the W share among
  the ascertainable papers (W + M + X). The U papers are reported separately and
  are not redistributed.
